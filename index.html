Container Inspector Support

Container Inspector is an internal educational and training application developed for a small group of professionals working with shipping containers in the Amsterdam port environment.

The application is intended solely for learning, revision and preparation related to container inspection and repair training.

Container Inspector does not certify containers, make pass/fail decisions, authorize repairs, or replace professional inspection procedures, official training materials, owner policies or qualified instruction.
